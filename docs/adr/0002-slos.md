# ADR 0002: SLOs for payments-api

**Status:** accepted

| SLO | SLI | Target (30d) | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Availability | non-5xx / all requests on `/payments` and `/webhooks/provider` | 99.9% | 43 min of full outage |
| Latency | p95 < 300 ms on the same routes | 95% of 5-minute windows | |
| Correctness | invariant violations (`make invariants`) | 0, always | none |

Correctness is not a percentage. A duplicate charge is an incident, full stop.

Availability is higher than the orders service in my other project (99.5%)
because a failed payment is lost revenue and a support ticket. The price is
less room for risky changes, which is why releases go through canary analysis
with thresholds stricter than the SLO.

Alerts (`deploy/platform/alerts.yaml`) are multi-window burn-rate:
page at 14.4× over 1h and 5m, ticket at 6× over 6h and 30m.
